Interactive Sankey Diagrams

This page provides interactive Sankey diagrams of recorded laying-hen transitions across four production stages: pre-, peak-, late- and end-laying. Diagrams are available for each physical housing section (P1–P5) and for pooled data. Both loop-inclusive and non-looping versions are provided.

Loop-inclusive diagrams allow self-loops (transitions from a location back to the same location), whereas non-looping diagrams exclude them. For clarity, approximately the top 20% of positive-count routes are displayed, ranked by transition count, with ties at the cutoff included. This refers to route types, not 20% of recorded transition events. An omitted route does not necessarily have a count of zero.

Link widths represent transitions per detected hen-day. Pooled rates are calculated by summing transition counts and detected hen-days across P1–P5 before division. Diagrams are scaled independently: use the numerical rates, rather than link widths, to compare activity between diagrams.

Hover over a link to inspect its origin, destination, raw transition count, rate and detected hen-day denominator. Hover over a node to inspect incoming and outgoing route counts, transition counts and rates. Node summaries distinguish the displayed links from all positive-count routes in the same representation before display filtering. Route counts describe distinct directed connections; transition counts describe recorded events.

In loop-inclusive diagrams, node summaries also report self-loop counts. Each self-loop contributes once to the incoming total and once to the outgoing total, so adding these totals would count self-loops twice. These summaries describe recorded transition activity, not the number of individual hens or time spent at a location.

Node colours identify locations consistently across diagrams, and each link takes the colour of its source node. P1–P5 denote physical housing sections. Because hens were rearranged between pre- and peak-laying, the same section label does not represent an unchanged group of hens across this boundary.
